2. What Are Cookies and Local Storage

Cookies are compact text strings that websites transmit to a visitor's device (typically the web browser), where they are stored and subsequently returned upon future visits.

Regulatory frameworks categorize storage mechanisms into:

  • Technical storage / cookies: strictly necessary to deliver the technical service or secure site navigation, exempt from prior consent requirements;
  • Analytics telemetry: used to measure audience size and page interactions in aggregate form;
  • Profiling and tracking cookies: designed to build persistent behavioral profiles for behavioral advertising across external domains (not used on this site).

3. Privacy-First Architecture: Zero Commercial Profiling Cookies

No Profiling or Third-Party Commercial Tracking:
This website does not install profiling cookies, employs no advertising trackers (e.g., Meta Pixel, Google Ads remarketing), and never sells or commercializes visitor telemetry.

Aggregate Telemetry Operations:
To understand scientific engagement with research manuscripts and open-source simulators, the site utilizes privacy-conscious aggregate telemetry:

  • No cross-site persistent tracking identifiers or advertising profiles are stored;
  • IP addresses are automatically masked and truncated before analytical aggregation;
  • No invasive canvas or browser fingerprinting is performed;
  • Outputs are strictly aggregated (e.g., page views, country-level geographic distribution, generic referrer categories, browser families).

Under Recital 25 of Directive 2002/58/EC and relevant European Data Protection Board (EDPB) guidance, non-profiling aggregate statistical analysis does not require intrusive consent banners.

4. Technical Local Storage (PWA / CacheStorage)

To enable progressive installation as a Progressive Web App (PWA) and guarantee offline availability of research materials, the site uses standard, secure browser APIs:

Technology Purpose Lifespan Classification
Service Worker (sw.js) Enables PWA installation and manages asset lifecycle updates. Persistent until application update or browser cache clear Strictly necessary technical
CacheStorage Caches HTML, CSS, and media assets locally for instant loading and offline availability. Managed locally by user browser Functional technical

These browser mechanisms do not transmit individual identifiers and operate solely within the user's local device sandbox.

5. Embedded Third-Party Media (YouTube & Instagram) — "Click-to-Play" Policy

Selected sections feature video frames and literary citations from YouTube and Instagram. To protect visitor privacy:

  • No third-party iframes or tracking scripts execute upon initial page load: all external media scripts remain blocked until voluntary interaction;
  • Embedded players instantiate only when the user explicitly clicks the preview card (e.g., "▶ Watch scene on YouTube" or "▣ Open Instagram frame");
  • YouTube video players are configured to load through the privacy-enhanced domain youtube-nocookie.com;
  • Upon clicking to activate a third-party player, the respective external platforms (Google Ireland Ltd. and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) may apply their standard platform policies and cookies.

6. Managing and Disabling Browser Storage

Visitors may inspect, restrict, or clear browser cookies and stored website data at any time through their browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies / Site data;
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection / Cookies and Site Data;
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Block all cookies / Privacy;
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Private browsing modes ("Incognito" / "Private Window") automatically wipe all session storage and cached files upon closing the window.
