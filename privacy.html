2. Scope, Types of Data Processed & Purpose

This website serves strictly non-profit academic research, scientific communication, and open-source dissemination purposes. It does not collect, record, or monetize personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive health data from visitors.

Aggregate Anonymous Telemetry:
The site utilizes aggregate analytics telemetry (Google Analytics 4 & Google Tag Manager) strictly to assess research reach and reader engagement. The service operates with automatic IP anonymization, does not sell or share data with advertising brokers, and avoids cross-site tracking or commercial profiling.

Data gathered in purely aggregate, non-identifying form includes:

  • Pages, publications, and repository links viewed;
  • Referrer source channels (e.g., GitHub, PubMed, social networks, search engines);
  • Device categories, general operating systems, and browser families;
  • Country and broad geographic area (without high-precision GPS or street-level tracking).

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage (PWA)

This website does not use advertising, marketing, or behavioral profiling cookies.

It relies on standard browser local cache storage (CacheStorage via Service Worker) solely to power its Progressive Web App (PWA) offline capabilities and allow offline caching of research resources. Such storage is strictly necessary to deliver the technical service requested by the user, exempted from cookie consent under Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive.

Third-Party Embedded Content (YouTube / Instagram): Embedded multimedia does not execute or autoplay upon page load. External embeds employ a strict click-to-play architecture, preventing pre-emptive third-party tracking until explicitly triggered by the user.

For comprehensive details on technical storage mechanisms and how to control browser data, please review our Cookie Policy.
